Ing. C. (Kees) van Beek is a technical staff member at TU Delft's Faculty of Civil Engineering and Geosciences. He works within the [Electronic and Mechanical Support Division (EMSD)](../demo), specifically the CE&G (Civil Engineering & Geosciences) unit — meaning he provides technical/instrumentation support to researchers rather than being a principal researcher himself. His role is based in room 23.S2 0.33.

His areas of expertise include:
- Sensors, instrumentation, and measurement systems
- Dynamics and test setup development

His publication record reflects this hands-on, experimental focus. Recent works include a 2022 paper on experimental ice basin tests with cylindrical structures (relevant to offshore foundations), a 2024 paper on a magnetic image sensor for detecting ferrous contaminants, work on capacitively coupled EM sensors, and a 2025 paper on an embedded ultrasonic tomography system for monitoring concrete structures.
In short, he appears to be a skilled technical engineer and instrumentation specialist who contributes to experimental research across several groups within the faculty, with a notable specialization in ice-related testing for offshore engineering applications.
