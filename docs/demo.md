title: DEMO
description: Electronic and Mechanical Support Division (EMSD)
---

**What it is:** DEMO is a department of TU Delft's University Corporate Office, alongside HR, Finance, Communications, and other support departments. The name DEMO stands for Dienst Elektronische en Mechanische Ontwikkeling (Department of Electronic and Mechanical Development).

**What it does:** DEMO facilitates experimental technical and scientific education and research through the rapid development and construction of unique experimental setups and prototypes. In practice, this means researchers across TU Delft can turn to DEMO when they need custom-built equipment, instruments, or rigs that can't just be bought off the shelf.

**Who it serves:** The DEMO organisation exists primarily to serve TU Delft, but where possible also offers its services to other educational and research institutions, knowledge companies, and high-tech start-ups.

**Its scale:** With a staff of approximately 85 and a modern, well-equipped range of machinery, DEMO is present at a number of faculties. It has locations embedded at several faculties, including Civil Engineering & Geosciences (CiTG), where Kees van Beek works.

Its capabilities span two main pillars — mechanical and electronic. On the mechanical side this includes precision machining, CNC milling and lathing, 3D printing, and welding. On the electronics side it covers instrumentation, data acquisition, sensor systems, control electronics, PCB assembly, and software development (e.g. LabVIEW).

So in short, DEMO/EMSD is TU Delft's in-house engineering workshop and technical support service — the people who actually build the experimental apparatus that researchers need to do their science.
