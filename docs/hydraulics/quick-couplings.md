# Quick change couplings
Hydraulic actuators are commonly connected using ISO 14541 (DN06 to DN20) compliant screw couplings.

Hydraulic actuator side:

| Connection | Gender | Marking | Manufacturer | MPN | Thread size |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| P | M | PVV DN20 M-D25 | DNP | PVV3.2019.113 | BSP 3/4 |
| T | F | PVV DN25 F-D25 | DNP | PVV3.2525.112 | BSP 1 |

Hose side:

| Connection | Gender | Marking | Manufacturer | MPN | Thread size |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| P | F | PVV DN20 F-D25 | DNP | PVV3.2019.112 | BSP 3/4 |
| T | M | PVV DN25 M-D25 | DNP | PVV3.2525.113 T | BSP 1 |

!!! warning
    Always put caps on unconnected couplings. If caps are damaged, replace them.
